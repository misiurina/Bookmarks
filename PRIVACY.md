# Privacy policy for Bookmarks Home

Bookmarks Home is a Chrome extension that shows and edits the bookmarks already stored in Chrome. It can also run in the side panel and, if you turn it on, as your new tab page.

This policy describes what the extension handles on your device. It does not send that information to the developer or to any other server.

## Information the extension handles

- Bookmark titles, addresses, and folder structure, through Chrome's bookmarks API. Creating, renaming, moving, and deleting items changes that same Chrome bookmark store.
- The title and address of the active tab, only when you use "Bookmark current page." The extension does not read your browsing history and does not watch tabs in the background.
- Site icons for bookmark addresses. Chrome supplies these through its favicon API. The extension does not download them itself or forward them anywhere.
- Preferences kept on this device: theme, toolbar icon color, expanded folders, hidden folders, saved searches, pinned favourites, and whether new tabs should open this extension. Favourites are a local shortcut. Pinning one does not create, move, or delete a Chrome bookmark.

## What it does not do

The extension has no account, no analytics, and no ads. It does not sell or transfer data. It does not use data for credit, lending, or advertising. It does not run code downloaded from the network.

## Storage and sharing

Bookmarks remain in Chrome. Preferences remain in local extension storage on this device. Nothing is transmitted to the developer. Chrome may contact a site to display its icon; that request is made by the browser, not sent to the developer.

You can remove local preferences by uninstalling the extension. Uninstalling does not delete your Chrome bookmarks.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address shown on the Chrome Web Store listing for Bookmarks Home.
