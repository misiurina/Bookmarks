Short description (also in manifest.json)

Easily find, organize, and open your bookmarks.

Detailed description

Organise and access your bookmarks in the best way possible.

Bookmarks Home lets you search, tidy, and open your bookmarks library beside the page you're visiting or on every new tab.

Here's what you can do:

• Search your library - Find a saved page by title or address 
• Pin favorites - Keep the links you use every day within reach 
• Pin folders as sections - See folders at a glance by managing them as sections
• Edit in place - Rename, move, add, and remove pages and folders 
• Save current page - Easily add the tab you're viewing to the right place in your library
• See folder as graph - Turn a long list into a graph to see the whole folder at a glance
• Make it your new tab - Make this extension your new tab page, or keep it beside you in the side panel

Perfect for anyone who wants to create and maintain a structure for their bookmarks.

Single purpose

Provide one place to browse, search, and edit Chrome bookmarks, in the side panel or, if the user turns it on, as the new tab page.

Workflow & Planning

Permission justifications

bookmarks — Read and change the user's Chrome bookmarks so they can browse, search, create, rename, move, and delete them.

favicon — Show the site icon next to each bookmark. Chrome loads the icon; the extension does not send the address anywhere.

sidePanel — Open the bookmark view in the side panel when the user clicks the toolbar icon.

storage — Remember the toolbar icon color and which folders were expanded during this browser session.

tabs — Read the title and address of the active tab only when the user saves the current page, and open a bookmark in a tab or window.

Data use

Do not disclose: personally identifiable information, health, financial, authentication, personal communications, location, web history, or user activity. The extension does not collect those.
